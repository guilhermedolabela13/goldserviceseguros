# Visual identity systems of leading global insurers and premium brokers (as of Oct 2026)

Scope: Lemonade, Hiscox, AXA, Allianz, Chubb, Zurich, Marsh (ex-Marsh McLennan), Howden, private-client players (PURE, Chubb Private Client). Goal: concrete inputs for a premium brand system for a Brazilian insurance advisory (navy #061C37 + gold).

## Typography: which typefaces, serif vs sans, hierarchy?

### Takeaway
The clearest recent move among majors is **pairing an editorial serif for headlines with a neutral sans for body**. AXA's 2025 evolution uses Publico Headline with Source Sans 3. Howden's 2023 rebrand uses GT Ultra, a face that blends serif and sans. Most others (Chubb, Zurich, Lemonade, Allianz) use custom or proprietary sans-serif wordmarks, and the foundries are rarely made public.

### Cited Findings
- **AXA (Brand Identity Evolution 2025)** uses two typefaces. Headlines are set in **Publico Headline** (a paid font from Commercial Type; Bold and Light styles required). Body and UI text use **Source Sans 3** (open source; Bold and Regular). — [AXA Design System](https://brand.designsystem.axa.com/brand-identity-evolution-2025/)
- **Howden (rebrand by North, Oct 2023)** uses **GT Ultra** from the Swiss foundry Grilli Type. It was chosen because it "combin[es] classical serif with modern sans-serif characteristics" to reflect the "personality of the business." The logotype has "chunky letterforms that blend together to create a distinct wordmark." — [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/north-howden-insurance-identity-branding/)
- **Zurich (72andSunny Amsterdam, 2020)**: "A new typeface system pairs the brand's heritage and expertise with their innovative, forward-thinking mindset… creates an ownable, dynamic system." The typeface name is not disclosed. — [PRINT Magazine](https://www.printmag.com/brand-of-the-day/brand-of-the-day-the-evolution-of-the-insurance-identity-thanks-to-72andsunny/)
- **Chubb**: the logo is a "custom drawn wordmark with no accompanying symbol." Its rules say it "should never be re-created, altered or set in a different typeface." — [Chubb Logo Usage Guide](https://news.chubb.com/usage-guide)
- **Hiscox**: a third-party logo database describes the wordmark as a "clean typeface that balances tradition with readability" but does not name the font. — [logotyp.us](https://logotyp.us/logo/hiscox/)
- **Lemonade**: a third-party source says it uses a contemporary sans-serif and that the custom font is not publicly identified. — [logotyp.us](https://logotyp.us/logo/lemonade/)

### Inferences
- Pairing a serif headline with a sans body (AXA's Publico plus Source Sans is a good template) is the most transferable pattern for a premium advisory. The serif signals editorial authority and heritage. The sans keeps the UI and small text legible. Options that fit navy and gold: a high-contrast serif such as Publico, GT Super, Canela or Tiempos for display, with Source Sans 3, Inter or similar for body. Fonts with full Latin/Portuguese diacritics support are essential.
- Hybrid serif/sans faces (GT Ultra at Howden) are a way to signal "heritage but modern" with a single family.

### Gaps
- Allianz's typeface could not be confirmed from a primary source. Search results point to a proprietary "Allianz Neo" (related to Neo Sans), but the Allianz Trade typography page returned 404 and no guideline confirmed it.
- No primary source confirmed Chubb's, Zurich's, Lemonade's or Hiscox's body typefaces.
- Marsh's 2026 typeface is not disclosed in the Lippincott or Transform coverage.

## Color: palettes, how trust and premium are signaled, dark backgrounds and gold accents

### Takeaway
The incumbents anchor on **one strongly owned core color**:
- **Blue:** AXA blue (with red), Zurich blue, and Marsh's new **midnight blue**.
- **Red:** Hiscox red (with dark gray).
- **Black/white:** the Chubb wordmark.
- **Pink:** Lemonade.
- **Teal:** PURE.

None of the brands researched uses metallic gold as a core color. Premium is signaled through restraint: dark navy or black, white space, and a monochrome logo.

### Cited Findings
- **Marsh (Jan 22, 2026, Lippincott)** uses **midnight blue** as the primary color, "building on the company's existing heritage branding while signaling a new era for its business brands." — [Lippincott](https://www.lippincott.com/ideas/marsh-works-with-lippincott-to-launch-new-masterbrand/); [Transform](https://www.transformmagazine.net/articles/2026/marsh-adopts-masterbrand-to-unite-four-businesses/)
- **AXA 2025:** the core colors are AXA Red, AXA Blue and AXA White, plus a tertiary palette reserved for data visualization. The design system page does not publish hex codes. — [AXA Design System](https://brand.designsystem.axa.com/brand-identity-evolution-2025/)
- **Chubb (2016):** the logo is black on light backgrounds and reversed (white) on dark backgrounds. — [Chubb Usage Guide](https://news.chubb.com/usage-guide)
- **Chubb (2016):** the brand launched with "nine distinct colors" representing diversity, energy, global presence, varied customers and multiple products. — [Chubb press release, 14 Jan 2016](https://investors.chubb.com/News--Events/news/news-details/2016/ACE-Completes-Acquisition-of-Chubb-Adopts-Chubb-Name-and-Launches-New-Chubb-Brand-01-14-2016/default.aspx)
- **Hiscox:** primary red **#da291c** is used on the corporate site. — [Hiscox Group "Our brand"](https://www.hiscoxgroup.com/about-hiscox/our-brand)
- **Hiscox:** a third-party source lists gray #231f20 and red #ee2d24, and describes the logic as "gray conveys maturity and red adds energy." This is not official. — [logotyp.us](https://logotyp.us/logo/hiscox/)
- **Zurich:** blue, with **#2b73c2** cited by a third-party source (unofficial). — [logotyp.us](https://logotyp.us/logo/zurich-insurance/)
- **Howden (2023)** deliberately avoided the "sombre colour tropes of the sector." It chose a "warm and human" palette of greens and khaki with bright reds and blues. — [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/north-howden-insurance-identity-branding/)
- **Lemonade:** pink is the signature color, and Lemonade won the right to use it in France in Dec 2020. — [Carrier Management](https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2020/12/18/214951.htm); [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/platforms/dtc-insurance-company-lemonade-uses-art-to-connect-with-its-youthful-customers/)
- **PURE Insurance (high-net-worth reciprocal):** primary brand color **#22b4b1** (teal), used as the meta theme color on its digital properties. — [PURE press kit](https://www.pureinsurance.com/press-kit)

### Inferences
- Navy #061C37 sits close to the "midnight blue" Marsh adopted in 2026. That is strong category validation for a trust/authority anchor.
- Gold is a differentiator in this category because no global major uses it as a core color. To stay premium rather than gaudy:
  - Treat gold as an **accent**: thin rules, numerals, small marks or a monogram, used on roughly ≤10% of a surface.
  - Use a muted or "antique" gold rather than a saturated yellow.
  - Use real metallic foil only in print and stationery. On screen, use a flat gold.
  - This follows the restraint seen at Chubb and Marsh, where a monochrome logo is reversed on dark grounds.
- Dark backgrounds (navy/black with a white logo) are standard in brand guidelines (see Chubb's reversed logo). These are reasonable defaults rather than documented "premium" claims.

### Gaps
- No official hex values were found for AXA, Allianz, Marsh's midnight blue, Chubb's nine colors or the Chubb Private Client palette.
- No documented example was found of a top-tier insurer using metallic gold as a brand color.

## Photography, illustration, iconography, grids, logo usage, motion

### Takeaway
The majors are treating **photography as a primary brand asset** (AXA 2025) and are building **flexible logo devices** that act as frames or windows:
- Zurich's "Safe Space" lettermark.
- Howden's "transparent lens."
- Marsh's converging-arrow "M."

Challengers like Lemonade lean on commissioned art and a single color.

### Cited Findings
- **AXA 2025:**
  - It keeps "the same logo, flexible and iconic."
  - Photography is elevated to a "distinctive brand asset."
  - Illustration works two ways: classic illustrations reimagined in the primary palette, and new abstract visuals.
  - Motion includes a "Hero Switch" and logo animations.
  - The work rests on three pillars: Building, Real, Confidence.
  - The goal is to feel "more refined, more modern."
  - — [AXA Design System](https://brand.designsystem.axa.com/brand-identity-evolution-2025/)
- **Zurich (2020)**: "Safe Space" was "created by adding functionality to the brand's lettermark to give room for people's ideas and dreams." The system uses geometric shapes, bold visual language and animated GIFs. — [PRINT](https://www.printmag.com/brand-of-the-day/brand-of-the-day-the-evolution-of-the-insurance-identity-thanks-to-72andsunny/)
- **Howden (2023)**: a "transparent lens" device signifies that "insurance is always there but invisible." Imagery seen through it includes North Sea wind farms and cyber security, and friendly illustrations add warmth. — [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/north-howden-insurance-identity-branding/)
- **Marsh (2026)**: the "M" symbol has two halves converging into a single upward arrow, representing "connection and collaboration." The launch campaign line is "Perspective Powers Progress" across out-of-home, print and broadcast. — [Lippincott](https://www.lippincott.com/ideas/marsh-works-with-lippincott-to-launch-new-masterbrand/)
- **Chubb logo rules:**
  - Clear space is two "B" heights vertically and two "B" widths horizontally.
  - Minimum size is 72 px digital and 25 mm print.
  - No symbol is used.
  - — [Chubb Usage Guide](https://news.chubb.com/usage-guide)
- **Chubb's** signature mark "Chubb. Insured." is framed as a craftsperson's mark of accountability, not a tagline. — [Chubb press release 2016](https://investors.chubb.com/News--Events/news/news-details/2016/ACE-Completes-Acquisition-of-Chubb-Adopts-Chubb-Name-and-Launches-New-Chubb-Brand-01-14-2016/default.aspx)
- **Allianz brand relaunch (2021)**, by DMC Group, Allianz's agency since 2001:
  - It was a "complete overhaul of the entire visual brand identity."
  - It added a new global visual language, a digital design system, motion and animation design, and an image database with a globally defined style.
  - — [DMC Group](https://www.dmcgroup.eu/en/project/branding-and-visual-identity-allianz/)
- **Lemonade's art and illustration:**
  - It used everyday objects "dunked" in signature pink.
  - It commissioned 30 emerging artists from summer 2020 for Instagram, reaching 17M views.
  - Head of Creative Nuno Ferreira: "No one wants to follow an insurance company on Instagram." — [AdExchanger, Feb 2021](https://www.adexchanger.com/platforms/dtc-insurance-company-lemonade-uses-art-to-connect-with-its-youthful-customers/)
- **Hiscox** builds its brand around art. "Art is an integral part of the culture of Hiscox" (a corporate art collection), alongside "distinctive marketing" that challenges convention. Its brand book dates from 2017. — [Hiscox Group](https://www.hiscoxgroup.com/about-hiscox/our-brand)

### Inferences
- For a premium advisory, three transferable patterns stand out:
  - A simple monogram or wordmark with strict clear-space rules (Chubb).
  - A brand device that can frame photography (Zurich and Howden).
  - Calm, real-life photography rather than stock clichés (AXA).
- An art or culture association (Hiscox) is a credible premium cue for private clients.

### Gaps
- No published grid specs were found for any of these brands.
- Iconography guidelines were not found in accessible sources.
- Motion specs beyond "logo animation / Hero Switch" were not found.

## Recent rebrands (2018–2026) and stated rationale

### Takeaway
The dominant theme is **masterbrand consolidation and simplification**:
- Marsh in 2026.
- Howden in 2023.
- Chubb in 2016 (before the 2018–2026 window, but still the reference).

The other recent identities are modernizations of existing brands:
- AXA in 2025.
- Allianz in 2021.
- Zurich in 2020.

### Cited Findings
- **Marsh** (masterbrand launched 22 Jan 2026 at Davos, agency Lippincott):
  - Marsh McLennan became "Marsh," with the ticker changing to MRSH.
  - Guy Carpenter becomes Marsh Re, and Oliver Wyman becomes "Oliver Wyman, a Marsh business."
  - Business units transition through 2027.
  - CEO John Doyle: "clients are seeking advice, solutions and insights that draw on expertise across our firm."
  - Lippincott's Allen Gove: "create a unified brand… while preserving the heritage of its legacy businesses."
  - — [Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2025/10/14/843566.htm); [Lippincott](https://www.lippincott.com/ideas/marsh-works-with-lippincott-to-launch-new-masterbrand/)
- **Howden** (Oct 2023, agency North): the rebrand unified its consumer and corporate businesses to "stand out from its many competitors." Howden Tiger became Howden Re (2024). — [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/north-howden-insurance-identity-branding/); [Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2024/04/04/767970.htm)
- **AXA** (2025 "Brand Identity Evolution", agency not credited on the system page): an evolution that keeps the logo, adds a serif and sans pairing, and elevates photography. — [AXA Design System](https://brand.designsystem.axa.com/brand-identity-evolution-2025/)
- **Allianz** (2021 relaunch, by DMC Group): a full visual identity overhaul. Allianz was #1 insurance brand in Interbrand Best Global Brands 2024. — [DMC Group](https://www.dmcgroup.eu/en/project/branding-and-visual-identity-allianz/); [Allianz](https://www.allianz.com/en/mediacenter/news/media-releases/241010-interbrand-best-global-brands-allianz-is-best-insurance-brand.html)
- **Zurich** (2020, 72andSunny Amsterdam): the rebrand places customers at the center rather than following industry conventions. — [PRINT](https://www.printmag.com/brand-of-the-day/brand-of-the-day-the-evolution-of-the-insurance-identity-thanks-to-72andsunny/)
- **Chubb** (14 Jan 2016, after ACE acquired Chubb):
  - The logo is "a simple, refined, modern expression of Chubb."
  - Chubb: "130 years of heritage… excellence, quality and service."
  - — [Chubb press release](https://investors.chubb.com/News--Events/news/news-details/2016/ACE-Completes-Acquisition-of-Chubb-Adopts-Chubb-Name-and-Launches-New-Chubb-Brand-01-14-2016/default.aspx)
- **Hiscox Re** rebranded and launched Hiscox Capital Partners (Feb 2026). No visual details were found. — [FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/02/23/hiscox-re-rebrands-and-launches-hiscox-capital-partners/)

### Inferences
- The stated rationales consistently stress simplicity, heritage preserved alongside modernity, and the client at the center. These phrases can serve as positioning language for the Brazilian advisory.

### Gaps
- No Pentagram or Wolff Olins insurance case among the named brands was found within this budget.
- The Brand New (UnderConsideration) reviews were not retrieved.
- AXA's 2025 agency is not identified.

## Premium/private-client brands vs mass-market digital insurers

### Takeaway
The two groups differ in four ways:

| | Premium / B2B / private-client (Chubb, Marsh, Hiscox, PURE) | Mass-market digital (Lemonade) |
|---|---|---|
| **Color** | One deep or neutral color (black, midnight blue) | A single loud signature color (pink) |
| **Logo** | Strict wordmark governance | Playful, social-first expression |
| **Associations** | Heritage and craft language ("Chubb. Insured.") | Commissioned art and humor |
| **Typography** | Increasingly a serif voice | Not compared in the sources found |

### Cited Findings
- **Chubb:** monochrome custom wordmark, strict usage rules, heritage messaging. — [Chubb Usage Guide](https://news.chubb.com/usage-guide); [Chubb 2016](https://investors.chubb.com/News--Events/news/news-details/2016/ACE-Completes-Acquisition-of-Chubb-Adopts-Chubb-Name-and-Launches-New-Chubb-Brand-01-14-2016/default.aspx)
- **PURE:** member-centric positioning with a teal primary (#22b4b1). PURE manages $3B+ premium and has 115,000+ members, a 95% renewal rate and NPS 51 (2024). — [PURE press kit](https://www.pureinsurance.com/press-kit)
- **Lemonade:** a pink signature color and an art-led Instagram presence, aimed at audiences under 35. — [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/platforms/dtc-insurance-company-lemonade-uses-art-to-connect-with-its-youthful-customers/)
- **Howden** is an outlier: a premium broker that chose warm, non-sombre colors to stand apart. — [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/north-howden-insurance-identity-branding/)

### Inferences
- For a Brazilian premium advisory, navy #061C37 with restrained gold matches the premium pattern: a dark authoritative core, a monochrome logo that reverses cleanly to white or gold, and serif display type. Gold gives a distinct cue that no major global competitor owns.

### Gaps
- No visual guidelines for Chubb Private Client as a distinct sub-brand were found.
- PURE's typeface was not found; its guidelines sit behind Zeroheight.
