# Brazilian regulatory and self-regulatory rules for insurance brokers' (corretoras de seguros) social media posts and advertising

Research date: 2026-10-06. Scope: Brazil; SUSEP/CNSP, the CONAR code, the CDC, the LGPD, trademark and copyright law, and broker associations (Fenacor, Sincor). Everything here is a research note and not legal advice. Where a statute is cited by its official Planalto URL without the page having been fetched in this session, that is marked "[statute text, not fetched this session]".

## Q1. SUSEP/CNSP rules on advertising by insurance brokers and on insurance products (Res. CNSP 382/2020, registration number, insurer disclosure, product names): what is current as of 2026?

### Takeaway
No SUSEP rule I found is written specifically for brokers' social media. The binding sources are these:
- **Res. CNSP 382/2020** (conduct rules): offers and advertising must be clear and adequate, and the broker must disclose the insurer(s) it represents, any exclusivity, and its remuneration.
- **The denominação rule**: "Corretora/Corretagem de Seguros" must appear in the firm's name and, according to SUSEP's 2011 enforcement reading, in all publicity.
- **Product circulars** (Circ. SUSEP 621/2021 for seguros de danos, Circ. SUSEP 667/2022 for seguros de pessoas):
  - Insurers must supervise all promotional pieces.
  - Every piece of marketing material must carry the sentence "o registro do produto é automático e não representa aprovação ou recomendação por parte da Susep".
  - Plan names must not mislead.

### Cited Findings
- **Res. CNSP 382/2020, Art. 3º, §1º, V**: supervised entities and intermediaries must carry out the "oferta, promoção e divulgação de produtos e serviços de forma clara, adequada", minimising client misunderstanding. **Art. 3º, §1º, VI**: contractual information must be given in a "clara, tempestiva e apropriada" way. — [Res. CNSP 382/2020 (SUSEP)](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F21953)
- **Res. CNSP 382/2020, Art. 7º, III**: internal procedures must cover the "divulgação, promoção e publicidade de produtos e de serviços". — [Res. CNSP 382/2020](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F21953)
- **Res. CNSP 382/2020, Art. 4º, §1º**: before contracting, the intermediary must formally tell the client:
  - any ownership stake of 10% or more in supervised entities;
  - any exclusivity obligations, and the supervised entities it represents;
  - "o montante de sua remuneração pela intermediação do contrato", alongside the premium.
  **§§2–3**: ownership and exclusivity information may or must be published through marketing materials, official channels or websites. — [Res. CNSP 382/2020](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F21953)
- **Res. CNSP 382/2020, Art. 5º**: the purchase of an insurance product cannot be made a condition of buying another good or service (no tying). — [Res. CNSP 382/2020](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F21953)
- **Res. CNSP 382/2020, Art. 14** inserted Art. 77-D into Res. CNSP 243/2011, setting fines of R$10,000 to R$500,000 for breaches of the conduct rules. — [Res. CNSP 382/2020](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F21953)
- Industry guidance on Res. 382 was published by Fenacor (a "nota de instrução") and IBRACOR, and the press reported that SUSEP would start penalising non-compliant brokers. These pages were not fetched. — [Fenacor](https://www.fenacor.org.br/noticias/resolucao-382-fenacor-divulga-nota-de-instruc); [ISB Brasil](https://isbbrasil.org.br/susep-comecara-a-punir-corretores-de-seguros-que-nao-cumprirem-dispositivos-da-resolucao-382-20/); [IBRACOR](http://ibracor.org.br/resolucao-cnsp-n-382/2020)
- **Circ. SUSEP 127/2000, Art. 8**: a brokerage firm's legal name and/or trade name must contain "Corretora de Seguros" or "Corretagem de Seguros", even when combined with other activities. The circular itself contains no explicit advertising provision. — [Circ. SUSEP 127/2000](https://www2.susep.gov.br/textos/circ127.htm)
- **SUSEP enforcement reading (2011)**: SUSEP warned that the expression must also appear in all publicity material, campaigns and client documents, internet domains and website content, so that brokers are not confused with insurers. Breaches lead to administrative proceedings and fines. — [CQCS, "Susep adverte..."](https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/susep-adverte-sobre-material-de-divulgacao-de-corretores/); [CQCS, "Corretor é proibido de usar 'seguros'..."](https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/corretor-e-proibido-de-usar-seguros-em-seu-material-de-divulgacao/)
- **SUSEP's current FAQ** for broker licensing still says the corporate name or business designation must include "Corretor(a) de Seguros" or "Corretagem de Seguros". — [SUSEP FAQ (gov.br)](https://www.gov.br/susep/pt-br/arquivos/arquivos-licenciamento/corretor-de-seguros/perguntas-frequentes)
- **Circ. SUSEP 621/2021** (seguros de danos):
  - **Art. 3**: "As peças promocionais e de publicidade deverão ser divulgadas sob supervisão da sociedade seguradora, respeitadas rigorosamente as condições contratuais e a regulamentação de práticas de conduta". The insurer is responsible for the accuracy of advertising.
  - **Art. 5, II**: "o registro do produto é automático e não representa aprovação ou recomendação por parte da Susep". **Art. 5, §2º**: this sentence must appear in all marketing and advertising materials.
  - **Art. 5, III**: the insured may check the registration status of the broker and the insurer at www.susep.gov.br.
  - Only Art. 63, XII was revoked, by Circ. 682 (effective 01/01/2025). Arts. 3 and 5 were shown as unamended.
  — [Circ. SUSEP 621/2021 (LegisWeb)](https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=409508)
- **Circ. SUSEP 667/2022** (seguros de pessoas, in force since 1 Aug 2022):
  - **Art. 6**: plan names, including any nome fantasia, "não deverá induzir os segurados a erro".
  - **Art. 7**: promotional pieces must be released under the insurer's supervision.
  - **Art. 10, II–III**: the same "registro automático... não representa aprovação ou recomendação" sentence, and the same pointer to check the broker and insurer at www.susep.gov.br.
  — [Circ. SUSEP 667/2022](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F26148)
- **Circ. SUSEP 656/2022** (títulos de capitalização; relevant only if the corretora promotes capitalização):
  - **Art. 30**: ads must show the company's razão social and the número(s) do(s) processo(s) SUSEP.
  - **Art. 31**: SUSEP's name or image may not be used except as the circular provides.
  - **Art. 32**: the name of the sociedade de capitalização must appear "em destaque".
  — [Circ. SUSEP 656/2022](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F25810)
- **Lei 15.040/2024** (new insurance contract law), in force one year after publication on 10 Dec 2024, so from about Dec 2025 (**Art. 134**):
  - **Art. 39**: brokers must deliver the documents entrusted to them within 5 business days.
  - **Art. 48**: exclusions and restrictions must be clearly written and highlighted.
  - **Art. 57**: doubts arising from the insurer's documents are resolved in favour of the insured.
  — [Lei 15.040/2024 (Planalto)](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2024/lei/l15040.htm)

### Inferences
- Because product circulars require the "registro automático... não representa aprovação" sentence in "all marketing materials", it applies whenever a post advertises a specific insurer's product. Brand-only or educational posts arguably do not trigger it, but including it is low-cost.
- Because promotional pieces must be released "sob supervisão da sociedade seguradora", a post that describes a specific product's coverages, prices or features should be checked by, or based on material from, the insurer. In practice insurers supply approved creatives through their broker portals.
- Name the insurer and the product (with the SUSEP process number if quoted) when advertising a specific product. Do not present the corretora as if it were the insurer (Circ. 127, Art. 8, and SUSEP's 2011 guidance).
- Showing the corretora's SUSEP registration number together with "consulte a situação cadastral em www.susep.gov.br" fits Arts. 5, III / 10, III. I found no norm that expressly requires the registration number in every social post. It is near-universal market practice, so treat it as strongly recommended rather than proven mandatory.
- Under Res. 382, Art. 4, §§2–3, remuneration and exclusivity are disclosed before contracting, not in ads. Exclusivity or ownership ties can be disclosed on the website or in the bio link.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether Circ. SUSEP 127/2000 has been formally replaced by a newer broker-registration norm. A 2012 Circ. SUSEP 429 and later norms exist but were not checked. The denominação requirement is still in SUSEP's current FAQ, so the substance is current.
- I could not confirm whether Res. CNSP 382/2020 was amended in 2024–2026. No revocation was found.
- I found no SUSEP norm or guidance specific to social media or influencers for brokers.
- Lei Complementar 213/2025 and other 2025–2026 reforms were not researched.

## Q2. Promising coverage or prices, "melhor seguro" claims, comparisons and testimonials: the CONAR code and the CDC on misleading advertising

### Takeaway
The CDC binds the advertiser to any sufficiently precise offer, including prices quoted in a post. It bans misleading advertising, including by omission of essential data, and puts the burden of proving claims on the advertiser. The CONAR code requires claims to be truthful and verifiable, allows comparisons only if objective and verifiable, and requires content to be identifiable as advertising. CONAR has no insurance-specific annex.

### Cited Findings
- **CONAR Code, Art. 1**: "Todo anúncio deve ser respeitador e conformar-se às leis do país; deve, ainda, ser honesto e verdadeiro." **Art. 23**: ads must not abuse consumer trust or exploit inexperience or lack of knowledge. — [CONAR Code (copy hosted by Migalhas)](https://www.migalhas.com.br/arquivo_artigo/art20130201-20.pdf)
- **CONAR Art. 27 (apresentação verdadeira)**:
  - descriptions, claims and comparisons must be verifiable;
  - ads may not deceive about the product's nature or purpose;
  - prices must be transparent and not exaggerated.
  — [CONAR Code](https://www.migalhas.com.br/arquivo_artigo/art20130201-20.pdf)
- **CONAR Art. 28**: "O anúncio deve ser claramente distinguido como tal, seja qual for a sua forma ou meio de veiculação". This is the basis for labelling paid partnerships and influencer posts. — [CONAR Code](https://www.migalhas.com.br/arquivo_artigo/art20130201-20.pdf)
- **CONAR Art. 32**: comparative advertising is allowed only when it is objective and verifiable, respects intellectual property and does not amount to unfair competition. — [CONAR Code](https://www.migalhas.com.br/arquivo_artigo/art20130201-20.pdf)
- **CONAR annexes**: Anexo E covers investments, loans and capital markets, not insurance. There is no dedicated insurance annex. Anexo Q covers testimonials, attestations and endorsements; its text was not in the copy fetched. — [CONAR Code](https://www.migalhas.com.br/arquivo_artigo/art20130201-20.pdf)
- The current CONAR code is the 2025 edition, with a separate Anexo X on betting (2023). The PDF links returned 404 or 403 during this session. — [CONAR 2025 (link)](http://conar.org.br/pdf/Codigo-CONAR-2025.pdf); [CONAR Anexo X](http://www.conar.org.br/pdf/CONAR-ANEXO-X-PUBLICIDADE-APOSTAS-dezembro-2023.pdf)
- **CDC (Lei 8.078/1990)** [statute text, not fetched this session]:
  - **Art. 30**: any sufficiently precise information or advertising binds the supplier and becomes part of the contract.
  - **Art. 31**: the offer must give correct, clear, precise and conspicuous information in Portuguese.
  - **Art. 36**: advertising must be easily and immediately identifiable as such, and the advertiser must keep the supporting data.
  - **Art. 37**: misleading advertising (including by omission of an essential fact, §3) and abusive advertising are prohibited.
  - **Art. 38**: the burden of proving the truth of advertising lies with the sponsor.
  - **Arts. 67–68**: making misleading advertising is a crime.
  — [CDC (Planalto)](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l8078compilado.htm); [CDC 2025 (Procon-SP)](https://www.procon.sp.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/CDC_2025.pdf)
- The TJDFT summarises its case law as holding suppliers liable for offers made on the internet. — [TJDFT](https://www.tjdft.jus.br/consultas/jurisprudencia/jurisprudencia-em-temas/cdc-na-visao-do-tjdft-1/o-consumidor-na-internet/responsabilidade-do-fornecedor-por-oferta-de-produto-na-internet)
- Influencer marketing can create civil liability under the CDC for misleading endorsements (doctrinal analysis). — [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/coluna/migalhas-de-responsabilidade-civil/418406/marketing-de-influencia-responsabilidade-civil-e-engano-no-cdc)

### Inferences (practical do's and don'ts)
- **Don't** promise "cobertura total", "indenização garantida", "aprovação garantida" or "sem burocracia" as absolute claims:
  - acceptance is always subject to risk analysis (Circ. 621, Art. 5, I);
  - exclusions and franquias apply;
  - under CDC Art. 30 an imprecise promise can bind the advertiser.
- **Don't** publish a price ("a partir de R$ X/mês") without its conditions: profile, region, vehicle or age, insurer, date, and "sujeito a análise de risco e condições gerais". Omitting essential conditions is misleading by omission (CDC Art. 37, §3).
- **Don't** use "o melhor seguro", "o mais barato" or "a menor franquia do mercado" unless you can substantiate them (CONAR Art. 27; CDC Art. 38).
- **Don't** compare insurers or competitor brokers unless the comparison is objective, verifiable and fair (CONAR Art. 32). Prefer "compare cotações de várias seguradoras" to rankings.
- **Do** keep evidence (quotations, sources, dates) for every factual claim (CDC Art. 36, sole paragraph; Art. 38).
- **Do** label paid partnerships and sponsored posts as advertising ("#publi", the paid-partnership tag) (CONAR Art. 28; CDC Art. 36).
- **Do** keep testimonials genuine and representative. Don't present an unusual claim payout as typical, and don't script fake reviews. This rests on Anexo Q principles, whose text I did not verify.

### Gaps
- The full text of CONAR Anexo Q (testimonials) was not retrieved; the CONAR site returned 403/404.
- I found no CONAR decisions (representações) specifically about insurance brokers.

## Q3. Use of insurer logos and brands by brokers; LGPD considerations for testimonials and client cases

### Takeaway
Insurer logos are registered trademarks and copyrighted artwork. Use them only with authorisation, usually through the insurer's partner or broker brand guidelines, and never in a way that suggests the broker is the insurer. Client testimonials, photos and claim stories are personal data, sometimes sensitive (health in life insurance), and need specific, documented consent plus image-rights authorisation.

### Cited Findings
- A Sincor-DF director and lawyer (Dorival Alves de Sousa, 16 Apr 2024) warned brokers:
  - not to use insurers' photographs, logos or images in advertising without explicit written authorisation;
  - commercial use of third-party works requires prior, express authorisation under Lei 9.610/1998.
  He cited a TJDFT case where an agency paid R$4,500 in copyright damages and R$4,500 in moral damages for using images without authorisation. — [SEGS / Sincor-DF](https://www.segs.com.br/seguros/394268-alerta-aos-corretores-de-seguros-o-uso-responsavel-de-imagens-e-logomarcas-em-publicidades)
- **Lei 9.279/1996** (industrial property): Art. 129 gives the owner exclusive use of a registered mark, and Art. 189 makes unauthorised reproduction or imitation of a registered mark a crime. [statute text, not fetched this session] — [Lei 9.279 (Planalto)](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l9279.htm)
- **Circ. SUSEP 656/2022, Art. 31** forbids using SUSEP's name or image in capitalização ads except as the circular provides. — [Circ. SUSEP 656/2022](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F25810)
- The Sincor-SP LGPD FAQ for brokers (2025) says:
  - consent must be express, through clear contract terms; generic authorisations are void;
  - mailings must offer opt-out and full deletion on request;
  - informal "pode me chamar no WhatsApp" consent should be formalised;
  - sensitive data (health, genetic, biometric) needs heightened protection.
  — [Sincor-SP LGPD FAQ](https://sincor.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/seminario_lgpd_cartilha_faq.pdf)
- **LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018)** [statute text, not fetched this session]:
  - **Art. 7**: legal bases for processing, including consent.
  - **Art. 8**: consent must be written or otherwise demonstrable, with a specific purpose.
  - **Art. 11**: sensitive data, including health data, may be processed only on stricter bases, typically specific and highlighted consent.
  - **Art. 18**: data-subject rights, including revoking consent.
  — [LGPD (Planalto)](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm)

### Inferences
- **Do** use insurer logos only from the insurer's official kit or broker portal, following its brand manual. Use them as "seguradoras parceiras" and keep them visually subordinate to the corretora's own brand.
- **Don't** alter insurer logos, or combine them so the post looks like the insurer's own ad.
- **Don't** use SUSEP's logo as an endorsement seal.
- **Don't** use stock or third-party photos without a licence (Lei 9.610).
- **Do** get, for testimonials and client cases:
  - a written, specific consent and image or voice authorisation (Civil Code art. 20 also protects image; not fetched);
  - consent that names the channels (Instagram, LinkedIn) and the duration;
  - a revocation path (LGPD Art. 18), with the post taken down on request.
- **Don't** reveal claim details, illnesses, deaths or policy numbers. For life and health stories use anonymised cases or explicit, highlighted consent (LGPD Art. 11). Anonymised or fictional "case" posts should be labelled as illustrative.
- **Don't** tag or name clients, including corporate clients on LinkedIn, without authorisation. B2B confidentiality clauses may also apply.

### Gaps
- I did not research specific insurers' published brand-usage policies for brokers (e.g. Porto, Bradesco, SulAmérica). Practice varies, so check each partner's broker portal.
- I found no ANPD decision specifically on insurance-broker marketing.

## Q4. Disclaimers and footers commonly used by Brazilian corretoras

### Takeaway
A defensible footer or bio for a corretora's posts and profile combines:
- the regulated denominação ("Corretora de Seguros");
- the razão social, CNPJ and SUSEP registration number, with a pointer to check it at susep.gov.br;
- when a product is advertised: the insurer's name, the product or process number, "sujeito à análise de risco / condições gerais", and the SUSEP "registro automático" sentence.

### Cited Findings
- Mandatory in all marketing and advertising material for insurance products (seguros de danos and seguros de pessoas): "O registro do produto é automático e não representa aprovação ou recomendação por parte da Susep." — [Circ. SUSEP 621/2021, Art. 5, §2º](https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=409508); [Circ. SUSEP 667/2022, Art. 10](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F26148)
- Standard wording in proposals and conditions, which the market reuses in ads: "A aceitação da proposta de seguro está sujeita à análise do risco." — [Circ. SUSEP 621/2021, Art. 5, I](https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=409508)
- Standard pointer: "O segurado poderá consultar a situação cadastral do corretor de seguros e da sociedade seguradora no sítio eletrônico www.susep.gov.br." — [Circ. SUSEP 667/2022, Art. 10, III](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F26148)
- "Corretora de Seguros" or "Corretagem de Seguros" must appear in the firm's name and, per SUSEP's 2011 warning, in publicity. — [CQCS](https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/susep-adverte-sobre-material-de-divulgacao-de-corretores/); [SUSEP FAQ](https://www.gov.br/susep/pt-br/arquivos/arquivos-licenciamento/corretor-de-seguros/perguntas-frequentes)
- Capitalização ads only: the razão social of the capitalização company and the SUSEP process number(s); "É proibida a venda de título de capitalização a menores de dezesseis anos"; "Antes de contratar, consulte previamente as condições gerais". — [Circ. SUSEP 656/2022, Art. 30](https://www2.susep.gov.br/safe/scripts/bnweb/bnmapi.exe?router=upload%2F25810)

### Inferences (suggested template, adapted from the rules above; not an official model)
- **Profile bio / LinkedIn "Sobre"**: "[Nome] Corretora de Seguros Ltda. | CNPJ xx.xxx.xxx/0001-xx | SUSEP nº xxxxxx | Consulte nosso registro em www.susep.gov.br".
- **Product post caption footer**: "Seguro [produto] da [Seguradora S.A.], Processo SUSEP nº xxxxx.xxxxxx/xxxx-xx. Aceitação sujeita à análise de risco. Consulte as condições gerais antes de contratar. O registro do produto é automático e não representa aprovação ou recomendação por parte da Susep. [Corretora] Corretora de Seguros – SUSEP nº xxxxxx."
- **Price posts**: "Valores ilustrativos, calculados em [data] para o perfil [X]; sujeitos a alteração e análise de risco."
- **Educational or brand-only posts**: the bio identification is generally enough. Adding the short "Corretora de Seguros – SUSEP nº" in the image or caption is a low-cost safeguard.

### Gaps
- I found no Fenacor or Sincor published template for social media footers. The templates above are inferred from SUSEP product-circular wording and market practice.
- I could not verify whether SUSEP has formally required the broker's registration number in ads. Treat it as best practice, flagged as uncertain.
