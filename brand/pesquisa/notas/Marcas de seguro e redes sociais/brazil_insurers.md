# Brand identity and social media of Brazilian insurers, insurtechs and brokers (as of Oct 2026)

Method note: the research ran on web search plus fetches of trade press (CQCS, Revista Cobertura, SEGS, Adnews, Propmark, Cidade Marketing, Marcas pelo Mundo) and one brand blog (Pier). Several Revista Apólice pages and one Meio & Mensagem page would not load (robots or timeout). For those, only the headline is cited and labeled "headline only". I could not open live Instagram or LinkedIn profiles, so I have **no verified follower counts** for any brand (see Gaps).

## 1. Visual identity and rebrands: how they signal trust vs. modernity

### Takeaway
The incumbents signal trust with a heritage name and a single strong brand color, and signal modernity with simpler names, "lighter" identities and humor-led campaigns with celebrities. Porto (2022) is the clearest example. The insurtechs keep blue as a trust anchor but add a warmer second color, softer palettes, serif and sans pairings, and a human "member" tone. Pier (pink, Calistoga + Heebo) is the documented case.

### Cited Findings
**Porto (formerly Porto Seguro)**
- April 2022: the company dropped "Seguro" and became "Porto", a nickname consumers already used. It adopted a "lighter, more contemporary" visual identity and restructured into three verticals: Porto Seguros, Porto Saúde and Porto Seguro Bank. — [Cidade Marketing](https://www.cidademarketing.com.br/marketing/2022/04/26/em-campanha-de-rebranding-porto-seguro-e-porto/)
- The launch campaign was by GUT São Paulo and premiered during *Big Brother Brasil* on 26 Apr 2022. It ran on TV, social, digital, OOH and radio with a humorous, fast-talking narrator. The concept line was "Quem tem Porto, tem tudo" (roughly "with Porto, you have everything"), and the tone was described as "leve e divertido" ("light and fun"). — [Cidade Marketing](https://www.cidademarketing.com.br/marketing/2022/04/26/em-campanha-de-rebranding-porto-seguro-e-porto/)
- The rebrand included social media activations by W3haus (headline only). — [Revista Apólice](https://revistaapolice.com.br/2022/04/rebranding-da-porto-inclui-acoes-de-redes-sociais-da-w3haus/); [SEGS](https://www.segs.com.br/seguros/342591-rebranding-da-porto-inclui-acoes-de-redes-sociais-da-w3haus)
- I could not verify Porto's exact hex colors or typeface from the sources I fetched. Porto's blue is widely known, but no fetched source confirms it.

**SulAmérica**
- December 2013: SulAmérica presented a new logo and visual identity, with an approach "increasingly focused on insurance brokers" (CEO Gabriel Portella). The article gives no colors, typeface or agency. — [CQCS](https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/sulamerica-apresenta-nova-identidade-visual-ao-mercado/)
- The public brand manuals ("Território de Marca – SulAmérica 2.0" and "Manual da Marca") are hosted on the SulAmérica Saúde site and would be the primary source for its palette and typography. I did not parse them. — [Território de Marca PDF](https://saude.sulamericaseguros.com.br/data/files/19/F2/8A/3C/9B271810CA17DF08F84616A8/Territ_rio%20de%20Marca%20-%20Sul%20Am_rica%202.0%20_1_.pdf); [Manual da Marca PDF](https://saude.sulamericaseguros.com.br/data/files/FB/50/72/00/FEB67510E8AEB6756AB916A8/Manual_da_Marca_SulAm_rica.pdf)
- SulAmérica used its 130th anniversary as a "new chapter" brand narrative, in a sponsored brand story (heritage as the trust signal). — [NeoFeed](https://neofeed.com.br/brand-stories/apresentado-por-sulamerica/sulamerica-completa-130-anos-e-inaugura-novo-capitulo-para-os-proximos-130/en/)

**Pier (insurtech)**
- The rebrand ran from August 2019 and launched in 2020. Pier kept blue "for trust/security" but put pink/rose forward as the brand's essence and added a pastel palette. It replaced Montserrat with **Calistoga (serif) + Heebo (sans)** and kept the logo unchanged. Brand Gym handled strategy and personality; Sweet & Co did the visual identity, including 2D and 3D systems. The tone is warm and human, with a manifesto co-created with members. — [Blog da Pier](https://blog.pier.digital/como-foi-o-rebranding-da-pier/)
- Pier calls its customers "membros" and announced reaching 100,000 members (headline only). — [SEGS](https://www.segs.com.br/seguros/339982-pier-seguradora-alcanca-a-marca-de-100-mil-membros-mudando-a-relacao-das-pessoas-com-o-mundo-dos-seguros)

**Youse**
- Youse's branding and visual identity was selected for the 12th ADG Design Biennial (page title only). — [12ª Bienal ADG](http://12.bienaladg.org.br/selecionados/branding-marca-e-identidade-visual-youse)

**Allianz Brasil**
- August 2026: new positioning, "Quem tem Allianz está pronto para viver o melhor" ("With Allianz you're ready to live your best"). The campaign was by iD\TBWA (creative) with media by WPP Media Brasil, starring Fernanda Lima. Its visual device is "frozen" scenes of calm moments across Auto, Home and Life, framing insurance as enabling good moments rather than protecting against loss. Marketing spend grew 30%+ year on year in 2024-25 and again in 2025-26, supporting a goal to double revenue by 2027. — [Adnews](https://adnews.com.br/post/allianz-lanca-campanha-estrelada-por-fernanda-lima-e-assina-novo-posicionamento-de-protecao-com-a-i-d-tbwa)

### Inferences
- Common pattern: incumbents keep their core color and logo equity (trust) and get their "modern" signal from a shorter name (Porto), humor and celebrity casting. Insurtechs get modernity from a warm second color (Pier's pink), editorial serif typefaces and community language ("membros").
- No major player found in this research positions on navy + gold. The incumbents' territories are blue, red and orange. That is my own recollection, not verified here, so check it against the brand manuals.

### Gaps
- No verified hex codes or typefaces for Porto, Bradesco Seguros, Tokio Marine, Allianz, SulAmérica (manuals not parsed), Youse, Justos, Azos, Minuto or Thinkseg.
- No source found on recent (2024–2026) identity changes for Youse, Justos, Azos or Minuto Seguros.

## 2. Social and campaign approach per brand: pillars, formats, tone, campaigns, cadence

### Takeaway
Big insurers run emotional or humorous 360° campaigns built around celebrities (Bradesco: ET and Tony Ramos; Tokio: Felipe Massa; Allianz: Fernanda Lima) and push them into social. The newer move is to humanize with people: Tokio Marine's employee creators (Dec 2025) and Minuto Seguros' content-creator team (2023). Insurtechs lean on transparency, speed and "desconfiança" (distrust) humor. Published engagement metrics for these brands are essentially absent.

### Cited Findings
**Tokio Marine Brasil**
- "Tokio Creators" (December 2025): 12 commercial-area employees from different regions make Instagram videos for the broker-facing account **@corretoretokio**. Topics are launches, service improvements, commercial campaigns and training. The rationale: "Pessoas se conectam com pessoas" ("people connect with people"), for more humanized communication with the distribution network. No metrics were disclosed. — [Revista Cobertura](https://revistacobertura.com.br/noticias/seguros-gerais/colaboradores-ganham-protagonismo-na-criacao-de-conteudo-para-redes-sociais-da-tokio-marine/); [CQCS](https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/colaboradores-ganham-protagonismo-na-criacao-de-conteudo-para-redes-sociais-da-tokio-marine/)
- "Seja Importante" campaign (1 Jul 2025, agency Santa Clara): Felipe Massa becomes "Felipe Márcio", an ordinary customer, to make the point that every customer matters equally. Formats were 30s and 15s spots, radio, Band News and Stock Car sponsorships, social, streaming, programmatic and influencers. The tone is humorous. — [SEGS](https://www.segs.com.br/seguros/424142-nova-campanha-tokio-marine-com-felipe-massa-humor-e-foco-total-na-protecao-do-cliente)
- A recurring brand line is "Com Tokio Marine, você nunca está sozinho" ("With Tokio Marine you're never alone"; headline only). — [SEGS](https://www.segs.com.br/seguros/399698-com-tokio-marine-voce-nunca-esta-sozinho-tranquilidade-garantida)

**Bradesco Seguros**
- "A Busca" (2024, AlmapBBDO): a girl befriends an alien. It is an emotional end-of-year story, "being beside people in all moments", and won Marketing Best 2025. No metrics were disclosed. — [CQCS](https://www.cqcs.com.br/noticia/grupo-bradesco-seguros-e-destaque-no-marketing-best-2025-com-a-busca/); [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/comunicacao/campanha-bradesco-seguros)
- Bradesco brought back its "Vai que…" ("What if…") tagline with Tadeu Schmidt (headline only). — [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/comunicacao/bradesco-seguros-retoma-bordao-vai-que-com-tadeu-schmidt)
- Tony Ramos returned to advertising for Bradesco Saúde (headline only). — [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/marketing/tony-ramos-volta-a-publicidade-com-bradesco-seguros)
- August 2026: a new campaign on microinsurance (headline only). — [Revista Apólice](https://revistaapolice.com.br/2026/08/bradesco-seguros-destaca-microsseguros-em-nova-campanha/)

**Allianz Brasil**
- 2026: the Fernanda Lima / "viver o melhor" 360° campaign. — [Adnews](https://adnews.com.br/post/allianz-lanca-campanha-estrelada-por-fernanda-lima-e-assina-novo-posicionamento-de-protecao-com-a-i-d-tbwa)
- An earlier activation used real customer stories of care and protection, and another made the customer the face of the brand ("Você, garoto propaganda da Allianz", roughly "You're Allianz's poster boy"). Both are headline only. — [Marcas pelo Mundo](https://marcaspelomundo.com.br/anunciantes/allianz-seguros-apresenta-acao-com-historias-reais-de-cuidado-e-protecao/); [CQCS](https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/voce-garoto-propaganda-da-allianz-seguros/)

- Only an older item on starting national brand promotion was found (headline only). — [SEGS](https://www.segs.com.br/seguros/62825-thinkseg-inicia-divulgacao-nacional-da-sua-marca.html)

**Format and cadence benchmarks (Brazil, all categories, not insurance-specific)**
- A HypeAuditor + Metricool study covered 83M posts from 3.5M Brazilian creator accounts between Jun 2025 and Jun 2026. Carousels can beat Reels on reach and likes, especially for accounts above 1M followers. Reels lead on comments and discovery. Accounts posting 2–3 times a week grew followers 26%, versus 93% for those posting 10+ times a week. The audience skews 25–34 (43%) and female (61% of reach). — [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/digital/reels-ou-carrossel-qual-gera-mais-engajamento-no-instagram-em-2026/)

### Inferences
- Shared pillars across brands: (a) emotional "beside you" or "never alone" stories, (b) humor that defuses distrust of insurers, (c) "people" faces (celebrities, employees, creators), (d) broker-facing channels as a separate line (@corretoretokio, Justos broker incentives, SulAmérica's broker focus).
- Insurance-specific engagement data is not public. The best available format proxy is the generic Brazil study: carousels for saves and reach, Reels for discovery.

### Gaps
- Follower counts for any brand on Instagram or LinkedIn: not verifiable (profile pages were not fetched).
- Posting cadence per brand: no published source.
- No agency case study with engagement KPIs (ER%, reach) for any of these insurers.
- No source covered Porto's or SulAmérica's Instagram editorial pillars specifically.

## 3. What successful independent corretoras do on Instagram

### Takeaway
The documented successes are simple, plain-language educational videos from the broker's own face. One example is Asserta (Brasília): a single video reached 5.8M views and generated quote requests nationwide. Industry guidance converges on education, behind the scenes, social proof and explained claims, but evidence beyond anecdote is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Asserta Corretora de Seguros (Brasília; Larissa Arruda) posts simple educational videos on why insurance matters, product explainers and loss prevention. One video hit **5.8M views** and generated quotes and clients from all over Brazil. Her quote: "Falo sobre seguros de uma forma bem simples com o objetivo de despertar no público a necessidade" ("I talk about insurance very simply, to make people feel the need for it"). — [CQCS, May 2022](https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/corretora-amplia-suas-vendas-usando-instagram/)
- Insurers now copy this broker-creator logic themselves: Tokio's employee creators and Minuto's creator team. — [Revista Cobertura](https://revistacobertura.com.br/noticias/seguros-gerais/colaboradores-ganham-protagonismo-na-criacao-de-conteudo-para-redes-sociais-da-tokio-marine/); [Insurtalks](https://www.insurtalks.com.br/posts/minuto-seguros-anuncia-novo-time-de-influenciadores-para-elucidar-mercado-de-seguros-no-brasil)
- There is a playbook and vendor literature for brokers (Agger, Lojacorr, Baeta's list of 11 Instagram profiles for brokers). It is promotional or advisory content, not data. — [Agger](https://www.agger.com.br/blog/instagram-para-corretoras/); [Lojacorr](https://redelojacorr.com.br/blog/como-divulgar-minha-corretora-de-seguros-nas-redes-sociais/); [Baeta](https://www.baeta.com.br/blog/perfis-no-instagram-para-corretor-de-seguros)

### Inferences
- What works: a founder or advisor on camera, plain language, one concrete risk per post, and a "peça sua cotação" ("request your quote") or direct-message CTA.
- Premium advisory brokerages (the family-office style "assessoria de seguros") appear to have little public social footprint documented in the press. That is itself a gap.

### Gaps
- No named premium or high-net-worth independent brokerage social case study was found.
- No quantitative study of broker Instagram content types in Brazil.

## 4. Gaps and opportunities for a premium navy + gold advisory

### Takeaway
The market is crowded with humor, celebrities and pastel or bright insurtech aesthetics. Nobody visible owns sober, editorial, "private-bank" advisory credibility on Instagram and LinkedIn. That leaves room for a navy + gold, serif-led, expert-led brand built on explained claims, risk analysis and discreet social proof.

### Cited Findings (evidence base for the opportunity)
- Incumbents get their modernity from humor and celebrities: Porto (GUT, "leve e divertido"), Tokio (Massa, humor), Youse ("Desconfiados" humor), Allianz (Fernanda Lima). — [Cidade Marketing](https://www.cidademarketing.com.br/marketing/2022/04/26/em-campanha-de-rebranding-porto-seguro-e-porto/); [SEGS](https://www.segs.com.br/seguros/424142-nova-campanha-tokio-marine-com-felipe-massa-humor-e-foco-total-na-protecao-do-cliente); [Marcas pelo Mundo](https://marcaspelomundo.com.br/anunciantes/youse-estreia-acao-para-fortalecer-a-confianca-e-entendimento-sobre-os-seguros/); [Adnews](https://adnews.com.br/post/allianz-lanca-campanha-estrelada-por-fernanda-lima-e-assina-novo-posicionamento-de-protecao-com-a-i-d-tbwa)
- Insurtechs go warm, pastel and pink, with a serif + sans pairing (Pier). — [Blog da Pier](https://blog.pier.digital/como-foi-o-rebranding-da-pier/)
- Distrust of insurers is explicit enough that Youse built a whole campaign on it. — [Marcas pelo Mundo](https://marcaspelomundo.com.br/anunciantes/youse-estreia-acao-para-fortalecer-a-confianca-e-entendimento-sobre-os-seguros/)
- "Pessoas se conectam com pessoas" is now insurer orthodoxy. — [Revista Cobertura](https://revistacobertura.com.br/noticias/seguros-gerais/colaboradores-ganham-protagonismo-na-criacao-de-conteudo-para-redes-sociais-da-tokio-marine/)
- Carousels are competitive on reach and saves in Brazil in 2026, and cadence correlates with growth. — [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/digital/reels-ou-carrossel-qual-gera-mais-engajamento-no-instagram-em-2026/)

### Inferences (opportunities)
- **Visual territory**: navy + gold with an editorial serif is distinct from the incumbents' bright brand colors and from insurtech pastels. It reads as heritage and discretion, like private banking. Use restrained photography of real advisors and clients' assets, not stock "family on the beach" images.
- **Editorial pillars to own**: (1) "Sinistro explicado": anonymized claim case files showing how the advisor fought for the client, the strongest answer to the distrust Youse exploits. (2) Risk analysis for high-value assets: homes, collections, D&O, life and succession planning (sucessão). (3) Policy "letras miúdas" (fine print) decoded in carousels. (4) Behind the scenes of the advisory process. (5) Discreet social proof: testimonials, years in business, insurer partnerships.
- **Format mix**: carousels for education and saves, short advisor-on-camera Reels for discovery (the Asserta model, made more premium), and LinkedIn long-form for B2B, D&O and wealth-planner audiences.
- **Tone**: calm, expert and precise, unlike incumbents' humor and insurtechs' playfulness. CTAs should be consultative ("agende uma análise de risco", "book a risk review") rather than "cote agora" ("get a quote now").
- **Cadence**: at least 3 posts a week, given the correlation between frequency and growth in the Brazil study.

### Gaps
- No direct premium or HNW broker comparables were found in the Brazilian press. Validate manually on Instagram by searching "assessoria de seguros", "family office seguros" and "seguro alta renda".
- No insurance-sector engagement benchmarks; the generic creator data may not transfer to brand accounts.
